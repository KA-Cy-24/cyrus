#### Bachelor’s Thesis: Towards microbial strigolactone biosynthesis in Bacillus subtilis through P450 enzyme engineering

- Modelled two MAX1 homologues from Arabidopsis and Oryza using I-TASSER, AlphaFold3 and Boltz; compared predicted folds with native B. subtilis P450s using TM-align.
- Mined analogous prokaryotic reactions and enzymes to identify candidate catalytic precedents.
- Preprocessed an existing literature-derived BM3 variant-reaction dataset.

#### Project: Representation of Functional Divergence with Site-constrained Protein Language Modelling

- Benchmarking global and site-restricted embedding strategies using pretrained ESM-2 and ESM-C across three experimental enzyme-specificity datasets (DAOx, AmiE and PafA).
- Evaluating embedding-based similarity measures and zero-shot masked-marginal log-likelihood ratios against experimental fitness measurements.
- Extracting residue-level embeddings across all Transformer layers to investigate layer-dependent representations and compare global, context-aware and active-site-focused approaches.
